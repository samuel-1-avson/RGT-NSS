# Privacy Policy v4

## 1. Data Collection

We collect customer usage data, billing information, and device statistics to improve service quality.

## 2. Customer Rights

Customers may request:

- Access to their data (free once per year).
- Deletion of account data upon termination (subject to financial record keeping laws).

## 3. Third Parties

We do not sell data to third parties. We share data only with authorized service providers for support and billing.
