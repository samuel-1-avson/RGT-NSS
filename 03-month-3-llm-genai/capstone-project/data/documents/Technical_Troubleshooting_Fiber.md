# Fiber Optics Troubleshooting Guide

## 1. Safety First

Ensure the customer is safe. Do not advise opening the ONT (Optical Network Terminal) box.

## 2. LED Status Indicators

### PON Light

- **Solid Green**: Normal operation.
- **Flashing Green**: Establishing connection.
- **Off**: No signal. Check fiber cable integrity.

### LOS (Loss of Signal) Light

- **Red (Flashing/Solid)**: Critical failure. Possible fiber cut or connector issue.
  - **Action**: Schedule technician immediately. Do not troubleshoot router settings.

## 3. Common Issues

- **Slow Speed**: Ask customer to disconnect other devices. Run speed test on wired connection.
- **No Internet**: Power cycle ONT and Router (wait 60 seconds).
