# Retention Policy 2025

## 1. Overview

This policy outlines the approved retention offers for customers at risk of churn. Use these offers based on customer tenure and value.

## 2. Eligibility Tiers

### Platinum (Tenure > 2 years, Monthly > $80)

- **Offer A**: $20 discount for 12 months.
- **Offer B**: Free upgrade to next internet speed tier.
- **Offer C**: One month free service (Manager Approval Required).

### Gold (Tenure > 1 year)

- **Offer A**: $10 discount for 6 months.
- **Offer B**: Free 3-month subscription to Streaming Service.

### Silver (Tenure < 1 year)

- **Offer A**: $5 discount for 3 months.
- **Offer B**: Waive late fees (up to 2 times).

## 3. Scripts

"We value your loyalty and want to ensure you're getting the best value. I can apply a [Offer] to your account immediately."
