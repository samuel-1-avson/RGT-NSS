# Billing Dispute Resolution Process

**Department:** Billing & Finance Support
**Applicable Regions:** Nationwide

## 1. Initial Review

When a customer disputes a charge:

1. **Identify**: Verify the specific line item on the bill.
2. **Clarify**: If the charge is valid (e.g., Pay-Per-View, Overage), explain the service usage.
   - Use `UsageLogs` to provide date/time of service.
3. **Escalate**: If the customer insists the charge is erroneous and amount > $50, escalate to Supervisor queue.

## 2. Adjustment Authority

- **Tier 1 Agent**: Can credit up to **$25 per interaction**. (Max $50 lifetime per account without approval).
- **Tier 2 / Supervisor**: Can credit up to **$200**.
- **Fraud Dept**: Unlimited authority for verified fraud cases.

## 3. Common Dispute Types

### 3.1 "I didn't order this movie"

- **Policy**: One-time courtesy credit allowed for first dispute. Warn about PIN usage for future purchases.
- **Action**: Credit amount + Activate Purchase PIN.

### 3.2 "My promotional rate ended without warning"

- **Policy**: Contract terms are sent via email 30 days prior. No retro-active credits mandatory.
- **Save Tactic**: If at risk of churn, offer current applicable Retention Offer (see `Retention_Offers_2025.md`).

### 3.3 Equipment Return Fees

- If customer has proof of return (shipping receipt): **Immediate Credit**.
- If no proof: Initiate "Lost in Transit" investigation (7-10 business days).
