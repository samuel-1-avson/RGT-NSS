# Device Upgrade Eligibility Program

**Program Name:** Telco Next Up
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024

## 1. Overview

Customers can upgrade their primary device (modem/router/mesh system) based on plan tier and equipment age.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria

- **Standard Upgrade**: Automatically eligible every 24 months.
- **Early Upgrade**: Eligible after 12 months with a one-time fee of $50.
- **Damaged Equipment**: Replacement cost is full retail value unless covered by `Device Protection Plan`.

## 3. Wi-Fi 6 / 6E Upgrades

Customers on legacy Wi-Fi 5 routers can upgrade to Wi-Fi 6 under the following conditions:

1. **Gigabit Plan**: Free upgrade.
2. **500Mbps Plan**: One-time fee of $99 or monthly rental increase of $5.
3. **< 500Mbps Plan**: Not eligible for Wi-Fi 6 router.

## 4. Return Process

- Old equipment must be returned within 14 days of receiving new device.
- **Non-Return Fee**: $150 will be charged if old device is not received. A prepaid shipping label is included in the new device box.
