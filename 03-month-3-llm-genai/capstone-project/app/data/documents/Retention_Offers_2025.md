# Retention Offers & Save Tactics (2025)

**Version:** 4.2
**Last Updated:** January 15, 2025
**Department:** Customer Success / Retention

## 1. Overview

This document outlines the approved retention offers and "save tactics" for customers expressing intent to cancel services. Agents must verify eligibility before extending these offers.

## 2. Eligibility Matrix

| Customer Tier | Tenure    | Monthly Spend | Primary Offer                     | Secondary Offer                           |

## 3. Specific Retention Scenarios

### 3.1 Competitor Price Match

**Scenario**: Customer claims "Comcast is cheaper."
**Action**:

1. Ask for proof of competitor offer (email/flyer).
2. If validated, match monthly rate for 12 months.
3. If unvalidated, offer **Code: SAVE10** (10% off for 6 months).

### 3.2 Service Reliability Complaints

**Scenario**: "My internet keeps dropping."
**Action**:

1. Check outage history in `NetworkOps` tool.
2. If verified outage > 4 hours in last month: Offer **Pro-rated Credit** for downtime + $20 "Apology Credit".
3. Schedule priority technician visit (Code: PRIORITY-TECH).

### 3.3 Moving / rRelocation

**Scenario**: "I'm moving to a new address."
**Action**:

1. Check service availability at new address.
2. Offer **Free Move** (waive $99 installation fee).
3. If new address is serviceable: Offer 1 month free to keep service.

## 4. Unauthorized Offers

The following are **strictly prohibited**:

- Permanent price reductions.
- Giving "New Customer" rates to existing customers (unless Tenure > 3 years).
- Waiving Equipment Return Fees for lost hardware.
