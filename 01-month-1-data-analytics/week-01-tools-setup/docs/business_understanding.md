# Business Understanding

## Problem Statement

Predict which customers are likely to churn (cancel their service) to enable proactive retention strategies and reduce revenue loss.

### Background

Customer churn is a critical business metric for telecommunications companies. Acquiring new customers is significantly more expensive than retaining existing ones. By identifying customers at risk of churning, the company can implement targeted retention campaigns.

### Business Objectives

1. **Primary**: Build a predictive model to identify customers at risk of churning
2. **Secondary**: Understand key factors that contribute to customer churn
3. **Tertiary**: Provide actionable insights for retention strategies

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest |
|-------------|------|----------|
| Marketing Team | Design retention campaigns | Target high-risk customers effectively |
| Customer Success | Manage customer relationships | Identify at-risk accounts early |
| Management | Strategic decision making | Reduce churn rate and improve retention |
| Data Science Team | Model development | Deliver accurate predictions |

## Success Criteria

### Quantitative Metrics

- **Model Performance**: Achieve >80% accuracy in churn prediction
- **Recall**: Identify at least 70% of customers who will actually churn
- **Precision**: At least 60% of predicted churners should actually churn
- **Business Impact**: Reduce churn rate by 5-10%

### Qualitative Goals

- Identify key drivers of churn
- Provide interpretable insights
- Enable proactive retention actions

## Business Questions

1. What are the main drivers of customer churn?
2. Which customer segments have the highest churn risk?
3. What is the financial impact of churn?
4. When are customers most likely to churn (tenure patterns)?
5. Which services are associated with higher churn rates?

## Constraints & Considerations

### Ethical Considerations
- Ensure fair treatment across demographic groups
- Avoid discriminatory targeting
- Respect customer privacy

### Technical Constraints
- Model must be interpretable for business stakeholders
- Predictions need to be available in near real-time
- Solution should integrate with existing CRM systems

## Expected Deliverables

1. **Trained Model**: Predictive model for churn classification
2. **Feature Analysis**: Key drivers and their impact on churn
3. **Dashboard**: Monitoring churn risk and model performance
4. **Recommendations**: Actionable retention strategies
